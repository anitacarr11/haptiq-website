The Concept

Dopamine design, not decoration.

Colour, texture and light change how a room feels — so here they're not an afterthought, they're the whole brief. Every space runs on a six-colour system anchored in deep aubergine: bold enough to photograph, considered enough to actually live in for a weekend.

As far as we've been able to find, it's one of the first spaces in Perth built this way on purpose — a short-stay apartment and a content creation studio, occupying the same one-bedroom footprint, designed together rather than one squeezed into the other.
